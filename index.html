Campground Horror

Can you discover what's making strange noises at the campground?

A camper reports that there have been some unusual noises and movements at the campground.

Your objectives:

  1. Talk to the camper and search the campsite.
  2. Investigate the shower house and collect a sample.
  3. Unlock the shed and solve the mystery.

Keep an eye on your flashlight battery while you explore.
